Your connection with NEWBORN K grows with every purchase. KREW is our loyalty program for shopping online and at our Kyiv concept store.

How to join

  1. Sign in to your NEWBORN K account.
  2. Shop online or in-store. Use the same email address and phone number so your purchases are linked to one profile.
  3. Build your lifetime purchase total and move up to the next level.

Membership is free. Your achieved level stays with you — no separate card or program activation is needed.

How to use your discount

  • Online: sign in before checking out and check your discount in the cart.
  • In-store: provide the email address or phone number linked to your profile before paying.
  • KREW discounts may not apply to specially priced items or products included in other promotions, unless stated otherwise.
